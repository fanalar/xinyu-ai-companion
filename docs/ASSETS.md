# Media and third-party resources

Original portrait photographs, generated videos, screenshots, recordings and business documents are not redistributed because provenance and redistribution permission were not established.
The included geometric SVG demo portraits are newly generated placeholders, licensed under this repository's MIT license. Replace them locally with assets you are authorized to distribute.
Speech models and Android libraries are downloaded separately and remain under their upstream licenses; the MIT license on this project's code does not relicense them.
